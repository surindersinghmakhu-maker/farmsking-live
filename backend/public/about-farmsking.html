🌍 1. National Launch Vision & Societal Purpose

FarmsKing™ (farmsking.com / farmsking.in) is officially launched at the National Level as a comprehensive digital agriculture, soil restoration, and direct marketplace platform. Built with strong alignment to Indian Government agricultural digitization standards, the platform serves as an indispensable boon for four major communities:

🌾 1. Progressive Farmers & Cultivators
Empowers farmers to reduce heavy synthetic chemical costs, access 100% free instant AI crop disease scans, adopt university-recommended package of practices, and sell directly to consumers and bulk buyers at fair market prices.
🏡 2. Urban Terrace & Balcony Gardeners
Provides doorstep organic potting mixes, vermicompost bio-fertilizers, compact micro-sprayers, and interactive mobile plant health care dosage reminders for urban families growing vegetables, flowers, and greens at home.
🥗 3. Organic Food Consumers & Nature Lovers
Direct farm-to-table access to verified chemical-free organic jaggery (gud), cold-pressed mustard oil, traditional pulses, basmati rice, and certified natural produce with complete traceability.
🏛️ 4. Governmental & University Alignment
Proactively aligned with Government of India natural farming policies, PAU (Punjab Agricultural University) agronomy standards, and APMC transparent market intelligence to strengthen India's rural economy.

🌱 3. Core Agricultural Domains & Offerings

🪱 Pure Vermicompost & Certified Bio-Fertilizers
Premium earthworm-processed organic vermicompost rich in active micro-organisms, humic acid, and nitrogen-fixing bacteria. Completely restores soil organic carbon, improves moisture holding capacity, and eliminates chemical soil degradation for field crops, horticulture, and urban gardens.
🌼 High-Yield Marigold (Floriculture) Cultivation
Specialized commercial floriculture program led by expert Sudhir Kumar Mahato. Provides complete agronomy blueprints for African and French marigolds, nursery spacing, pest deterrence, and direct festival market linkages for 3x to 5x higher net income per acre compared to traditional crops.
🩺 Multimodal AI Crop Doctor & Vision Pathology
Instant plant disease diagnosis in 820ms using Google Gemini Flash and specialized vision transformers. Farmers snap a photo of damaged leaves or stems to receive immediate biological causes, chemical remedies, organic treatments, and audio explanations in pure English, Punjabi, and Hindi.
🌾 Live Mandi Price Intelligence & Commodity Tickers
Daily price tickers, historical arrival graphs, and price discovery projections across APMC mandis for wheat, paddy, basmati, cotton, mustard, potato, onion, and seasonal vegetables to help farmers time their sales for peak profit.
🏡 Urban Balcony & Terrace Garden Care Kits
Custom-packaged urban garden care kits with enriched potting soil, organic neem sprays, micro-drip accessories, and digital dosage reminders for city residents and nature lovers cultivating organic vegetables and plants on balconies.
