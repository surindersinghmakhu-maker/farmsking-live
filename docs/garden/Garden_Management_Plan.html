Garden Management System
Implementation Plan

ISO Standard Document | Premium Web Application Architecture

This document outlines the architecture and features required to build the comprehensive "Garden Management" system, integrating the Gardener Dashboard and Garden Advisor modules.

1. Target Audience & Scope

  • Target Audience: Home Kitchen Gardeners, hobbyists with potted plants, and professional gardeners.
  • Core Value: Providing direct, continuous, and expert guidance for plant care, disease management, and correct dosing of fertilizers/medicines.

2. Kitchen Garden Profile & Setup

  • Onboarding Requirement: Users must add a minimum of 5 items/plants to initialize their Kitchen Garden profile.
  • Plant Details: For each plant, the user will input:
    • Plant Name / Type
    • Variety / Species
    • Location (e.g., Balcony, Backyard, Pot)
    • Date planted
  • Media Uploads: Users can upload continuous progress pictures of their plants for tracking.

3. Issue Reporting & Tracking

  • Problem Reporting: A dedicated feature to report issues (e.g., yellowing leaves, pests).
  • Direct Link to Advisor: When a problem is reported, it instantly alerts the subscribed Garden Advisor with all historical data (past pics, plant details).

4. Garden Advisor (Doctor/Guide) Integration

  • Automatic Suggestion Algorithm: Once a user adds their initial 5 items, the system will automatically recommend Advisors whose expertise matches the planted items.
  • Routine Scheduling & To-Do Lists: Advisors will create a recurring care calendar (e.g., watering schedules). Gardeners see this as a daily "To-Do List".
  • Seasonal Alerts: Push notifications for weather changes.
  • Prescription System: When a user reports an issue with a photo, the advisor can reply with a precise "Prescription" detailing medicine, dosage, and application method.
  • AI Garden Doctor (First Filter): Users can optionally use the AI Camera to scan a disease before messaging the human advisor.
  • E-Commerce Integration: Any medicine or seed recommended features a direct "Buy Now" button linked to the platform's Shop.

5. Subscription & 3-Tier Pricing Model

The platform will operate on a hybrid SaaS + Marketplace 3-tier model:

Tier 1: Free Plan (Platform Base)

  • Limits: Maximum of 5 plants lifetime (active slots).
  • AI Features: Limited AI Scans and limited AI Chats (e.g., 10-20 queries per week).
  • Lifecycle: When a plant dies or is harvested, its status is marked as "Lifecycle Complete".

Tier 2: Basic Plan (Platform Premium)

  • Limits: Unlimited plants can be added to the digital garden.
  • AI Features: Unlimited AI Scans and Unlimited AI Chats.
  • Revenue: Fixed monthly/yearly SaaS subscription fee directly to FarmsKing.

Tier 3: Advisor Plan (Service Marketplace)

  • Pricing: Advisors set their own customized fees. All advisor plans are listed in the marketplace.
  • Plant Adoption System: Even if a user has "Unlimited" plants, an Advisor will only "Adopt" and guide a specific number of plants (based on the fee).
  • Swap Requests: If a gardener wants the advisor to guide a different plant, they must send a "Change Adopted Plant Request" to the advisor for approval.

6. Advanced Engagement Features (To Be Developed Later)

  • Harvest & Savings Tracker: Users log harvested produce. The app calculates monetary savings based on local market rates.
  • Smart Weather Alerts: Predictive alerts for rain, frost, or heatwaves.
  • Community Showcase (Social Feed): A gallery to share photos of blooming flowers or large vegetables.
  • Gamification & Badges: Digital badges (e.g., "Master Gardener") for maintaining healthy plants.
  • Seed & Plant Exchange (Barter): A hyper-local feature to swap seeds or saplings for free.
  • Pest & Disease Outbreak Radar: Automated alert system warning gardeners if a pest is reported by multiple users in their city.
  • DIY Home Composting Guide: Tracking system for kitchen waste compost bins.
  • Kid's Corner (Family Gardening): Gamified sub-profile for children with simple tasks to earn digital stars.
  • Vacation Mode: Specific watering techniques before a user leaves for a trip, or alerting a neighbor.
  • Upcycling & DIY Planters: AI and Advisor tips on converting household plastic waste into pots.
  • 100% Organic Certificate: A digital certificate awarded to Kitchen Gardens logging no chemical pesticide usage for 6 months.
  • Garden Short Videos (Reels): A dedicated feed where Advisors post 60-second educational videos.
  • Plant Adoption & Rescue Center: A local marketplace to give away dying or unwanted plants for rescue.
  • Garden Layout Planner: AI-generated layout maps showing where to place pots for optimal sunlight.
  • Indoor Microgreens & Sprouts: A guide for users with zero outdoor space.
  • Monthly Gardening Challenges: Photo contests with rewards like free organic fertilizer.
  • Garden Scrapbook (Time-lapse): Stitch photos of plant growth into a shareable video.
  • Companion Planting Guide: Recommendations on which plants to grow together to naturally repel pests.
  • Eco & Water Savings Tracker: Display exact liters of water saved by following the advisor's schedule.
  • Pollinator Attractor Mode: Guidance on planting flowers to attract bees/butterflies for pollination.
  • Ready-to-Grow Seasonal Kits: Curated e-commerce kits that automatically add the correct items and an advisor to the profile.
  • Subscription Freeze/Pause: Allow users to pause their advisor subscription during non-gardening months.
  • Weed vs Plant AI Checker: AI tool to help identify and remove weeds growing alongside main plants.
  • Advisor Rating & Review System: 5-star rating and written review after subscription expires.
  • Drag-and-Drop Garden Grid Planner: Interactive tool to map out seed/pot placement.
  • Offline Accessibility: Ensure core features work seamlessly without internet connectivity.
  • B2B Nursery Integration: Partner with local nurseries for targeted ads or direct purchase links.

7. Platform Monetization (Direct FarmsKing Revenue)

  • Advisor Certification Course: "FarmsKing Certified Expert" course with a one-time certification fee.
  • FarmsKing Seller Products (Marketplace Integration): Direct prescriptions from verified Sellers generating marketplace commission.
  • Corporate "Green" Tie-ups: Bulk packages sold to IT companies.
  • Data Monetization & Targeted Ads: Aggregate user crop data to sell targeted advertising spots to major brands.
  • Premium Done-For-You Setup: Physical team sent to the user's house to set up their balcony garden for a premium fee.
  • Plant Replacement Insurance: Premium (e.g., ₹200) when buying a costly plant for free replacement if it dies within 3 months.
  • Soil Health Testing Kit: DIY soil testing kits connected to specific FarmsKing fertilizer recommendations.

8. Required UI/UX Updates

  • Terminology: Ensure all references strictly use "Gardener", "Garden", "Kitchen Garden", and "Garden Advisor".
  • New Screens to Build:
    1. Kitchen Garden Setup Wizard (to mandate the 5-plant minimum).
    2. Plant Detail & Media Gallery (for picture uploads).
    3. Report a Problem (ticket system linked to the advisor).
    4. Advisor Subscription Marketplace (to choose 6-month, yearly, or per-plant plans).
