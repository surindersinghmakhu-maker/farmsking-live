## 2. STATUTORY SCOPE & OPERATIONAL BOUNDARIES (e-MoA ALIGNMENT)

In accordance with the statutory Memorandum of Association (e-MoA) approved by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, FarmsKing operates under strict product and operational boundaries:

### Permitted Operational Scope (Active):
1. **Digital Agritech SaaS & Mobile Applications**: Farm cultivation logging, Bahi-Khata ledger, commission calculations, soil records.
2. **AI Plant Pathology & Tele-Agronomy**: Camera leaf disease detection, certified doctor consultation scheduling, spray advisory.
3. **APMC Mandi Price Intelligence**: Real-time modal price feeds, price predictions, mandi conversion voucher tracking.
4. **Certified Organic Bio-Inputs & Vermicompost**: Laboratory-tested organic fertilizers, bio-stimulants, and soil amendments.
5. **Live Nursery Plants & Saplings**: Direct doorstep delivery of healthy nursery saplings, flowering varieties, and medicinal herbs.
6. **Satellite Crop Telemetry**: Multispectral satellite remote sensing (NDVI, NDRE, NDMI).

### Prohibited & Excluded Operational Scope (Strictly Enforced):
- ❌ **Zero Hardware Sales**: The platform does NOT sell physical agricultural machinery, tractors, harvesters, or mechanical tools.
- ❌ **Zero Equipment Rental / Leasing**: The company does NOT lease, rent, or broker heavy farm machinery or implements.
- ❌ **Zero Machinery Manufacturing**: The company has zero involvement in manufacturing physical agricultural machinery or fabrication.
